Appendix B — Events

All 170 events in scope, all built and scored (15 October). The fold column is the cross-validation fold each event was scored in. They are grouped by continent, newest first. Country is the country at the event’s centre, so an event near a border can be listed under the neighbour (FL20180619BGD, a Bangladesh activation, falls in India).

  • map days: distinct dates with a gold map.
  • windows: separate map areas, each processed on its own.
  • cells: every 0.01° cell in the event’s windows (the rectangles around its maps), examined or not, floodable or not. A cell is about 1.1 km across at the equator.
  • gold flooded cell-days: floodable cells flooded on a map day, summed over the map days; these are the cells scored for recall. A 0 means the maps’ flood areas never covered 30% of a floodable cell.
  • fold: the cross-validation fold (1–5) the event was scored in; its rates were fitted on the other four folds. Events in one river basin share a fold.
